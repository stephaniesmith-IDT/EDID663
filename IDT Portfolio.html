01  ABOUT

I teach. I design. I'm still learning how the two fit together.

My path into instructional design is grounded in a simple conviction. Good learning is designed, not left to chance. I bring 21 years of experience in K-12 and higher education classrooms into everything I design, and I'm currently building on that foundation through Mercer University's ID&T program, where I'm learning to move from learning theory and needs analysis, through media production and LMS delivery, to assessment, accessibility, and program evaluation.

I design with alignment and empathy. Every objective earns its activity, every activity earns its assessment, and every learner, regardless of ability or background, has a way in. This portfolio is a living record of that practice, and it grows with every project.
